
# Christianity Infographic - Quick Reference

## ✝ CHRISTIANITY ✝
**Origins, Beliefs, and Global Spread**

---

### 📍 WHERE & WHEN
- **Time:** 1st century CE
- **Place:** Judea (modern Israel/Palestine)
- **Based on:** Life, teachings, death, and resurrection of Jesus of Nazareth
- **Timeline:** 1st C. (Jesus & Apostles) → 4th C. (Constantine) → Today (2+ Billion believers)

---

### 👥 KEY FIGURES
- **Jesus Christ** - Central figure, Son of God
- **Mary** - Mother of Jesus
- **Peter** - Lead apostle, early church leader
- **Paul** - Missionary to Gentiles
- **Constantine** - Roman emperor who legalized Christianity (313 CE)
- **Augustine** - Major theologian and bishop

---

### 🛣️ HOW IT SPREAD
**Routes:** Roman roads & Mediterranean trade networks

**Causes of Spread:**
- Missionary work by apostles & followers
- Trade merchants carrying teachings
- Military movement across empire
- Appeal of Christian message
- Imperial support after Constantine

---

### ⛪ BELIEFS & DEITIES
**Type:** Monotheistic (One God)

**The Trinity:**
1. God the Father
2. God the Son (Jesus Christ)
3. The Holy Spirit

*One God in three persons, NOT three separate gods*

---

### 🌍 CHRISTIANITY TODAY
- **Largest religion globally** - 2+ billion followers
- **Practiced worldwide** - Europe, Americas, Africa, Asia
- **Major branches** - Catholicism, Protestantism, Eastern Orthodoxy
- **Visible in culture** - Churches, Christmas, Easter, holidays
- **Major influence** - Social institutions & global values

---

### ⭐ INTERESTING FACT
**From Small Movement to World Religion:**

Christianity grew from a tiny Jewish sect in one region into a global faith with 2+ billion followers. Why? Through the Roman Empire's incredible road system, bustling trade routes, cities, and communication networks. When Constantine legalized it in 313 CE, it became the empire's official religion. A small group of disciples became a worldwide phenomenon—proving that geography, leadership, and infrastructure can completely transform the spread of ideas.
