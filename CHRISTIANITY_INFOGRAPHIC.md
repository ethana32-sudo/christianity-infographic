# ✝ CHRISTIANITY ✝

**📍 WHERE & WHEN:** 1st century, Judea (modern Israel)

**👥 KEY FIGURES:** Jesus Christ, Mary, Peter, Paul, Constantine, Augustine

**🛣️ HOW IT SPREAD:** Roman roads & Mediterranean trade routes

**⛪ DEITIES:** One God (The Trinity: Father, Son, Holy Spirit)

**🌍 TODAY:** 2+ billion followers worldwide (largest religion)

**⭐ COOL FACT:** Small Jewish sect → world religion through Rome's roads & Constantine's support (313 CE)
