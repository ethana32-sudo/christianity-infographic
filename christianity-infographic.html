Origins, Beliefs, and Global Spread

Place: Judea (modern Israel/Palestine), Roman province

👥 Key Figures

  • Jesus Christ: Central figure, Son of God
  • Mary: Mother of Jesus
  • Peter: Lead apostle, early church leader
  • Paul: Missionary to Gentiles
  • Constantine: Roman emperor who legalized Christianity (313 CE)
  • Augustine: Major theologian and bishop

🛣️ How It Spread

Routes: Roman roads & Mediterranean trade networks

Causes:

  • Missionary work by apostles & followers
  • Trade merchants carrying teachings
  • Military movement across empire
  • Appeal of Christian message
  • Imperial support after Constantine

⛪ Beliefs & Deities

Type: Monotheistic (One God)

The Trinity:

  • God the Father
  • God the Son (Jesus Christ)
  • The Holy Spirit

One God in three persons, NOT three separate gods

🌍 Christianity Today

  • Largest religion globally (2+ billion followers)
  • Practiced worldwide: Europe, Americas, Africa, Asia
  • Major branches: Catholicism, Protestantism, Orthodoxy
  • Visible in culture: churches, Christmas, Easter, holidays
  • Major influence on social institutions & values

⭐ Interesting Fact

🚀 From Small Movement to World Religion: Christianity grew from a tiny Jewish sect in one region into a global faith with 2+ billion followers. How? Through the Roman Empire's incredible road system, bustling trade routes, cities, and communication networks. When Constantine legalized it in 313 CE, it became the empire's official religion. A small group of disciples became a worldwide phenomenon—proving that geography, leadership, and infrastructure can completely transform the spread of ideas.
